Local disposal rules: City of Cambridge, MA (curbside, residential)
Source: City of Cambridge DPW, "Your guide to trash, recycle, compost, and more!" 2022/2023 guide (wastemailer2022.pdf)
and cambridgema.gov/services/recycling, read 17 Oct 2026. Not confirmed by the city for this project; used only to
SUGGEST a bin for a new item type, which a person approves before it counts.

RECYCLE (blue cart; recycling is mandatory in Massachusetts):
- Metal cans and foil.
- Glass bottles and jars.
- Plastic containers.
- Paper and cardboard (flatten boxes).
- Place items loose; do not bag recyclables.
- NO food or liquids: lightly rinse containers.
- NO plastic bags or plastic film (air pillows, plastic wrap, shrink wrap), NO wrappers.
- NO hoses, cables, straps, hangers, electronics.
- NO clothing and textiles. NO pots, pans, scrap metal.
- NO paper takeout containers, paper towels, napkins, or paper plates.

COMPOST (curbside compost or drop-off):
- All food scraps, even meat, bones, and shells; dairy, bread and grains, leftovers.
- Napkins and paper towels.
- Coffee grounds and filters, tea bags.
- Cut flowers. Compostable bags.

TRASH:
- Items that are not recyclable, not compostable, and not special items below.
- Trash should not contain food items.

SPECIAL (not curbside; Recycle Center, special collection, or hazardous waste event):
- Clothing and textiles (state law bans textiles in the trash): donate or textile recycling, even if torn or stained.
- Batteries (non-alkaline), small electronics, fluorescent bulbs, plastic bags, scrap metal, books: Recycle Center.
- Items labeled HAZARDOUS, POISON, DANGER, WARNING, or CAUTION (paints, chemicals, medications, propane tanks,
  car batteries): household hazardous waste event. Never put them in the trash.
